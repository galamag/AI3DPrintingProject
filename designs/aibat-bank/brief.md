## 1. What it is

A personalised money box in the style of the name banks in the references. It is a thick black block whose front and back both carry the name «АЙБАТ» as raised green capitals. Each side is a flat black face, and the five letters stand straight out of it by 4 mm, with upright walls and flat faces, like the THOMAS reference. The style is clean and toy-like: flat colours, crisp edges. A coin slot sits on top. The box splits down the middle into a front half and a back half, which click together and can be pried apart with a coin to take the money out.

**Surface finish, as accepted by the user.** Each half prints with its letter faces down on the textured plate. The green letter faces therefore get the fine plate texture. The flat black face is printed in mid-air, 4 mm above the plate, on supports that are snapped off afterwards. It comes out slightly rough and shows faint support marks, and it has no plate texture. The side walls and top show fine horizontal layer lines.

**One outline for both sides.** Seen from behind, the name's natural outline is the mirror image of the front one: the hump over Й sits on the other side, and so does the step under Т's arm. The two halves must meet on one shared outline, so the outline is made symmetric left to right: it is the front outline and its mirror image laid on top of each other, with the top between the two humps filled to one flat raised plateau. The result is a long block with a flat bottom, upright ends, rounded corners and a raised flat plateau over the middle three letters. Most letters have an 8 mm black border; in a few places the black face is wider (section 3.1). The user is to confirm this look from the renders.

Assumptions I made where the references and the user's answers say nothing:

- **Font: Impact**, which is installed on this PC (`C:\Windows\Fonts\impact.ttf`) and has Cyrillic letters. It is the closest installed match to the THOMAS letters: upright, heavy and narrow. It is a little heavier and narrower than THOMAS. Arial Black is also installed, but it is about 1.5 times wider and would have to be squeezed heavily to fit 220 x 100 mm.
- **Relief height 4.0 mm.** That is 20 layers of 0.2 mm. On 66 mm tall capitals it reads clearly from the side and in the three-quarter view and casts a visible shadow, like the THOMAS reference. A higher relief would make the supports taller, use more filament and make them harder to pull out of the letter holes; a lower one would look flat.
- **The 100 mm height includes the raised plateau.** The short mark on top of Й (its "breve") sits above the other letters, and the body wraps round it. The top of that wrap, extended across the middle as a flat plateau, is the top of the box at 100 mm. The two ends of the top are lower, at about 82 mm (estimate).
- **The 80 mm depth is measured from the front letter faces to the back letter faces** (from the correction), so it includes both 4 mm reliefs.
- **The back name is the front name turned half a turn round the vertical centre line.** From behind, the first А is on the viewer's left, as it is from the front. The two sides therefore look identical.
- **The name is centred by its width.** On each side the green word, from the left foot of the first А to the right tip of Т's top bar, is centred on the box. Because the back is the front turned round, both sides show the same placement.
- **The small wedge at each end is filled** (section 3.1, step 6), so each end is one straight upright edge. Without it each end would have a small notch where the leaning side of А meets the corner under Т's arm.
- **A small edge break** (a 0.6 mm 45 degree chamfer) runs round the outer edge of each flat black face, where it meets the side walls. It looks neater and hides the rough support edge a little.
- **To reach 220 x 100 mm, the letters are stretched upwards by about 15–25 %** (estimate). Impact's natural proportions are wider than 2.2 : 1 for this name. A stretch of up to 25 % keeps the font looking natural: it makes the horizontal strokes slightly thicker.
- **Letters do not touch.** They have a 3 mm gap, which shows as a thin black line of face, so each green letter reads on its own. In some of the references the letters overlap.
- **The coin:** 200 tenge, about 26 mm across (the user's figure). I assume no coin is thicker than 2.5 mm.
- **The banknote** is folded lengthwise into thirds or quarters before it goes in, to a strip no wider than about 28 mm. This assumes a banknote about 70 mm tall, which is my estimate. The 38 mm slot also takes most notes folded only in half if they are no more than about 72 mm tall; that is an estimate too.
- **The box is opened from below.** You turn it over, put a coin into a small notch at the bottom seam and twist.

The box is **2 printed parts**: the front half and the back half. A separate **test piece of the joint** has 4 small printed parts and is printed first. The scene therefore holds 8 objects in three groups. A "collection" is Blender's folder of objects. Supports are not modelled; the slicer adds them.

### 3.1 The shared body outline (both halves)

This is the shape every slice of the body follows from the flat black face to the seam. Both halves use exactly the same outline.

1. **Letters.** Set «АЙБАТ» in Impact capitals.
   - Adjust the spacing so that every pair of neighbours is exactly 3.0 mm apart at its closest point.
   - The breve keeps its own position over Й from the font.
2. **Fitting the size.** Do these steps in this order.
   1. Scale the whole word evenly until its green width is 204 mm, from the left foot of the first А to the right tip of Т's top bar.
   2. Stretch it upwards only, until its green height is 84 mm, from the baseline to the top of the breve.
   3. Limits: the upward stretch must lie between 1.00 and 1.25 times. If more than 1.25 is needed, use 1.25 and report the height that results. If the word is already taller than 84 mm at 204 mm wide, scale it evenly to 84 mm tall instead and report the width that results.
   4. **Placement (centring):** the baseline goes at Z 8, and the word's green width (step 1) is centred on X = 0, so the word runs from X −102 to +102. This is the front word. The back word is the same word turned half a turn round the vertical line X 0, Y 0, so it is centred the same way as seen from behind.
3. **Front outline.** Grow the outline of all the front letters, breve included, outwards by 8 mm, with round corners. Then:
   - fill every hole inside it (the insides of А and Б, the closed gaps between letters, the gap between the breve and Й);
   - cut it flat at Z = 0, and fill any dip in the bottom edge (under the legs of each А) up to that flat line.
4. **Mirror.** Add to it its own mirror image about X = 0, so the outline covers both. This is the outline the back word needs, seen from the front.
5. **Plateau.** There are now two humps on top: the one over the front Й (left of centre, at about X −42) and its mirror (at about X +42). Fill the top between them up to a flat line at the humps' height, Z 100, from the outer side of one hump to the outer side of the other. The two outer shoulders keep their round 8 mm corners. Result: one flat plateau, expected from about X −52 to +52 at Z 100, with a step down at about X ±60 to the end tops at about Z 82.
6. **Ends.** At each end, a small wedge-shaped notch is left where the leaning side of an А meets the rounded corner under a Т arm (about 4–8 mm deep, between Z 10 and Z 60, estimate). Fill it, so each end runs straight up at X ±110 from the rounded bottom corner to the rounded top corner.
7. **Any other dip** left in the top edge is filled to the straight lines of step 5. Inner corners where the shoulders meet the end tops may stay sharp or get a round of up to 2 mm.

The result is symmetric left to right, and spans X −110 to +110, Z 0 to 100. In plain words: **a long block with a flat bottom, upright ends, rounded corners, flat tops at about 82 mm over the first and last letters, and a flat plateau 18 mm higher over the middle three letters.**

### 3.2 `BankFront` (front half, with the front name and the lip)

**The front, from the letter faces back:**

- **Letter faces:** all five letters and the breve are flat, at Y −40, and parallel to the black face.
- **Letter walls:** each letter outline (and its holes) runs straight back, square to the face, for exactly 4.0 mm, to the flat black face at Y −36. No bevel, no slope, no rounding on the letters; the letter face edges are crisp.
- **Colour boundary:** there must be a real edge in the mesh at Y −36.4, 3.6 mm behind the letter faces, all round every letter wall and every hole wall. The letter faces and the walls in front of this edge are green; the last 0.4 mm of the walls behind it, and everything further back, is black. This is where the filament changes in the print (section 10).
- **Flat black face:** one flat surface at Y −36, filling the whole body outline round the letters: the borders, the wider areas of 3.1, the 3 mm gaps between letters, the insides of А and Б, and the gap between the breve and Й. It meets the letter walls in sharp inside corners.
- **Edge break:** round the outer edge of the flat black face, where it meets the side walls, a 0.6 mm 45 degree chamfer.
- **Side wall:** the body outline runs straight back from the black face to the seam at Y 0. It is 3.2 mm thick.
- **Inside:**
  - **Shape:** the hollow follows the body outline, shrunk by 3.2 mm, with corners rounded to at least 2 mm. Any part of the hollow narrower than 6 mm is left solid.
  - **Depth:** the hollow's front face is flat at Y −32.8, so the face wall behind the flat black face is 3.2 mm thick. Behind the letters it is 7.2 mm (4.0 mm relief plus 3.2 mm). The hollow is open towards the back (Y 0).
- **Seam line:** the outer edge of the rim (at Y 0) has a small 0.6 mm 45 degree bevel. With the matching one on the back half it makes a neat V line, so the seam looks intended.
- **Lip:** the inner 1.4 mm of the wall continues past the seam by 4.0 mm, to Y +4.0, all the way round, so it slides into the back half. Its inner face is flush with the inside wall. Its outer face is 1.8 mm in from the outside surface.
  - **Why the lip is on the front half:** the halves are otherwise twins, so the joint works either way. Keeping it on the front half keeps the joint exactly as on the test piece (`CouponFront` has the lip), keeps the pry notch beside the lip as designed, and leaves the back half 4 mm lower on the plate.
- **Click bumps (4):** small ridges on the outer face of the lip. Each is 8 mm long along the wall, with its ends tapered over 1 mm.
  - **Profile across the ridge:** it stands 0.4 mm out from the lip. It is 1.4 mm wide at the base (Y +1.8 to +3.2) and has a flat top 0.6 mm wide (Y +2.2 to +2.8), with 45 degree sides.
  - **Placement:** two on the bottom wall, at about X −50 and X +50. Two on the top wall, centred on the flat end tops at about X −85 and X +85 (over the first А and over Т).
  - **Spacing:** each bump is at least 10 mm from a corner, the slot and the pry notch.
- **Coin slot, front half:** a notch in the top wall of the plateau from Y −2.5 to the seam at Y 0, through the full wall into the hollow.
  - **Length:** 38 mm, with rounded ends (radius 2.5 mm), so the two halves together make a slot with round ends.
  - **Place:** centred at X 0 on the plateau, so it runs from X −19 to +19.
  - **Length rule:** use 38 mm if the flat plateau is at least 46 mm long, which is expected (about 104 mm). Otherwise, use the flat length minus 8 mm, but never less than 32 mm. That leaves at least 4 mm of flat top beyond each end of the slot. If even 32 mm does not fit, stop and report.
  - **Lip:** over the slot's length plus 2 mm at each end, the lip is left out.
- **Pry notch:** a notch for a coin, in the bottom wall at the seam, centred at X 0. It is 14 mm long (along X), 2.4 mm wide (Y −2.4 to 0) and 1.5 mm deep (Z 0 to 1.5). It is cut in the front half only. A coin pushed in bears against the back half's rim, and twisting it pries the halves apart.

### 3.3 `BankBack` (back half, with the back name)

- **Outside:** exactly the outside of `BankFront` from its letter faces to the seam, turned half a turn (180 degrees) round the vertical line X 0, Y 0. So:
  - the letter faces are at Y +40, and read «АЙБАТ» left to right when you look at the back, with the first А on the viewer's left (+X);
  - the letter walls run straight forward 4.0 mm to the flat black face at Y +36, with the colour-boundary edge at Y +36.4;
  - the flat black face, its 0.6 mm edge break, and the 3.2 mm side wall on the shared outline, forward to the seam at Y 0.
- **Inside:** the same hollow outline as the front half, with its back face flat at Y +32.8 (a 3.2 mm face wall). It is open towards the front.
- **Rebate for the lip:** the step in the wall that takes the front half's lip. Over the first 4.3 mm behind the seam (Y 0 to +4.3), only the outer 1.6 mm of the wall stays, as a thin skirt. The inner part is removed, which leaves a 0.2 mm gap round the lip's outer face. The lip stops 0.3 mm short of the floor of the step (Y +4.0 against +4.3), so the halves meet on the outer seam and not on the lip's end.
- **Click grooves (4):** in the skirt's inner face, opposite the 4 ridges. Each is 10 mm long and 0.3 mm deep.
  - **Profile:** it is 1.6 mm wide at the face (Y +1.7 to +3.3) and has a flat floor 1.0 mm wide (Y +2.0 to +3.0), with 45 degree sides.
  - **How it clicks:** when the halves close, each ridge has to push the skirt 0.2 mm outwards and then drops into its groove.
- **Coin slot, back half:** the matching notch, from the seam at Y 0 to Y +2.5. It has the same length, ends and place as in the front half. Over the slot's length plus 2 mm at each end, the rebate is filled solid (full wall thickness), because the lip is missing there.
- **Seam line:** the same 0.6 mm 45 degree bevel on the outer edge of the skirt's end.
- **No pry notch** on the back half.

## 6. Topology (how the surface is built)

- **Type:** hard-surface solids made for printing. Each object is one closed solid: watertight, no holes in the surface, no loose or doubled faces, no parts passing through themselves, normals pointing outwards.
- **Real size:** everything is built at the sizes above, with the scale applied (reset to 1).
- **No smoothing step** (no subdivision). Curved letter outlines are made of enough short straight pieces that no facet shows in the renders: no more than 0.05 mm off the true curve.
- **Size of the mesh:** about 50,000–200,000 triangles per half is fine.
- **Thinnest parts:**
  - nothing thinner than 1.2 mm anywhere. The thinnest parts are the 1.3 mm wall behind each click groove and the 1.4 mm lip;
  - the click ridge (0.4 mm high) and the groove (0.3 mm deep) are shapes on a wall, not free-standing details.
- **Colour boundary:** on both halves, the edge 3.6 mm behind the letter faces is a real edge round every letter wall and hole wall, so the faces in front of it can carry the green.
- No animation, no rig.

## 8. Lighting, background and camera for the presentation renders

- **Engine:** Cycles on the graphics card, 128 samples with denoising, 1600 x 1200 (landscape 4:3).
- **Background:** a light warm-grey seamless studio backdrop, #E6E3DE, with a soft shadow under the box. A black box on a dark background would vanish.
- **Lights:**
  - key: a large soft light to the front-left of the camera, 45 degrees to the side and 35 degrees up, so the letters cast short shadows on the black face;
  - fill: a weak light on the right, so the black face and the shadowed letter walls do not go flat;
  - rim: a light high behind the box, which draws a thin highlight along the black outline and the seam.
  - For the back views (`back` and `back-hero`), the whole light set turns with the camera, so the back is lit exactly as the front is.
- **Camera:** an 85 mm lens, so there is almost no distortion. It aims at the centre of the box (X 0, Y 0, Z 50). The box fills about 85 % of the frame width.

## 10. Print

- **What it is for, and the load:**
  - A money box on a desk or shelf. It stands on its bottom face and holds coins and folded notes.
  - Full, it may hold about 2–3 kg of coins (estimate). The weight rests on the bottom walls of both halves.
  - The lip inside the step stops the halves from sliding against each other up and down or sideways. The 4 clicks only hold them together.
  - It is a box for keeping money, not a lock: a child can open it with a coin.
  - Pick it up full by holding both halves, not by one half alone.
- **Printed size:**
  - assembled: 220 x 80 x 100 mm (W x D x H);
  - front half on the plate: 220 x 100 mm, 44 mm tall (40 mm plus the 4 mm lip);
  - back half on the plate: 220 x 100 mm, 40 mm tall;
  - each fits the 256 x 256 x 256 mm volume with about 18 mm to spare along the long side.
- **Plates, in order:**
  1. **Test piece** (`CouponFront`, `CouponBack_015`, `_020`, `_025`).
     - Grey, slot A2, one colour, no supports.
     - Each part lies floor-down.
     - Small and quick, about 15 g (estimate).
     - Its result sets the gap before the box is exported (3.4).
  2. **Front half** (`BankFront_Print`), letter faces down, with supports and one colour change.
  3. **Back half** (`BankBack_Print`), exactly the same way.
- **For both halves:**
  - **Bed face:** only the green letter faces and the breve touch the plate (Y −40 for the front half, Y +40 for the back half). They are large and flat and get the textured-plate finish.
  - **The colour change rule:** green (A1) from the plate; the slicer changes to black (A4) **two layers below the flat black face**, that is at the relief height minus two layer heights. With 0.2 mm layers and the 4.0 mm relief this is **3.6 mm**: green for layers from 0 to 3.6 mm, black from the layer that starts at 3.6 mm to the top. The model's colour-boundary edge sits at the same 3.6 mm. If a different layer height is chosen, the change and the boundary edge move to 4.0 mm minus two of those layers, and the modeler moves the edge to match.
  - **Why two layers early (the user's reason):** the last support layers (their top interface) and the last layers of the letter walls are then already black, so the black face is not smeared with green from the supports. The 0.4 mm black at the base of each letter is not noticeable from the front.
  - **Layer height** must make both 3.6 mm and 4.0 mm layer boundaries, for example 0.2 mm layers with a 0.2 mm first layer.
  - **Supports are required** under the whole flat black face, which lies 4.0 mm above the plate: round the letters, in the wider black areas, in the 3 mm gaps between letters, inside the holes of А and Б, and in the gap between the breve and Й. All supports stand on the plate; nothing inside the box needs support. The user's point, recorded: the supports are printed during the green layers, so they come out green (with black top layers) and cost no extra filament change or purge.
  - **For the print engineer to decide:** the support type (normal or tree), the interface layers and their pattern, the top Z gap between the support and the black face (it decides how cleanly the face releases and how rough it is), and whether the narrow 3 mm gaps and the small closed letter holes can be bridged instead of supported. Pulling supports out of the closed holes of А and Б is the awkward part of the clean-up; a support pattern that comes out in one piece helps.
  - If the slicer adds a purge tower for the colour change, the half lies so that the tower fits beside its 100 mm side.
  - Both halves change colour at the same height, so they could share one plate (about 220 x 210 mm together). Version 1 plans one plate each: then a failed plate costs only one half, and the purge tower has room. The print engineer may suggest one shared plate to the user.
- **Surface finish, accepted by the user on 2026-11-13:** the flat black faces are printed over supports, so they do not get the plate texture and will show support marks. Only the letter faces get the plate texture. This was accepted in exchange for straight letters.
- **Filament estimate** (rough; the print engineer gives the real figures). All Bambu PLA Lite, loaded as A1 green, A2 grey, A3 white, A4 black:
  - black (A4): about 270–340 g for both halves, including the black top layers of the supports;
  - green (A1): about 50–80 g for both halves: about 30–40 g in the letters and about 20–40 g in the supports, which are thrown away;
  - grey (A2): about 15 g for the test piece;
  - white (A3): not used;
  - plus the waste of the two colour changes (purge), a few grams to about 15 g each (estimate).
- **Overhangs:** in print orientation, the only surfaces facing the plate are the letter faces (on the plate) and the flat black face with its edge break (on the supports). Everything else is upright or leans no more than 45 degrees: the click ridges and the sides of the grooves are 45 degrees, the slot and the pry notch are open at the top of the rim, and there is no ceiling inside either half.
- **Walls:** outer walls 3.2 mm, face wall 3.2 mm behind each flat black face, lip 1.4 mm, skirt 1.6 mm, at least 1.3 mm behind a groove.
- **Fits and gaps, starting values:**

  | Fit | Starting value | Tuned by |
  |---|---|---|
  | Lip to skirt, sideways | 0.20 mm | test piece: 0.15 / 0.20 / 0.25 |
  | Lip end to the floor of the step | 0.30 mm | |
  | Click ridge | 0.4 mm high, so 0.2 mm overlap when passing the skirt | |
  | Ridge sitting in its groove | 0.1 mm free | |
  | Slot | 5.0 mm, for coins up to 2.5 mm thick and folded notes | |
  | Pry notch | 2.4 mm wide, for a coin edge | |

- **May not be changed without the user:**
  - the font, the name, the overall size, the name on both sides;
  - the straight letter walls, the 4.0 mm relief, and the colour change two layers below the black face;
  - the split at Y 0, the slot size and position, the colours, the shared outline once the user has confirmed it.
  - Turning either half to another face defeats the design. Report instead.
- **For the print engineer:**
  - The click joint needs solid walls near the rim. Four or more wall loops make the 3.2 mm walls nearly solid.
  - The letter faces of each half must touch the plate fully: flat, not tilted.
  - Whether `slice_model` can insert the filament change at 3.6 mm and set up the supports is not known. Either may have to be set in the Bambu Studio window, for two plates. This is a question for the orchestrator and the user, not a reason to change the model.

## 11. Acceptance criteria (checked by the inspector)

1. **Size and standing.**
   - The assembled box (`BankFront` + `BankBack`) measures 220 ± 1 mm in X, 80 ± 0.5 mm in Y (letter faces to letter faces) and 100 ± 1 mm in Z, with its bottom at Z = 0.
   - If the upward stretch was capped at 1.25, the height reported by the modeler applies instead, and the user is told.
   - The bottom face is flat at Z = 0 and contains a 190 x 68 mm rectangle centred on the origin. The centre of mass of the empty box lies above the middle third of that rectangle.
2. **Shared outline.**
   - The body outline is symmetric left to right within 0.1 mm.
   - The plateau is flat at Z 100 ± 0.5 mm for at least 90 mm, centred on X 0 within 1 mm.
   - Each end is a straight upright edge at X ±110 ± 0.5 mm between its rounded corners.
   - The modeler reports the plateau's length, the height of the end tops and where the shoulders are.
3. **Names on both sides.**
   - Render 1 (`front`) and render 4 (`back`) both read «АЙБАТ» left to right in Impact, not mirror-written, with all five letters and the breve green, and no missing-glyph boxes.
   - The outside of `BankBack` matches the outside of `BankFront` turned 180 degrees round the line X 0, Y 0, within 0.1 mm, from the letter faces to 5 mm before the seam.
   - The narrowest gap between each pair of neighbouring letters is 3.0 ± 0.3 mm; the upward stretch is between 1.00 and 1.25; the breve stands on the black face, separate from Й.
4. **Straight relief, on each side.**
   - All letter faces lie at Y −40.0 (front) and Y +40.0 (back), ± 0.05 mm.
   - The flat black face lies at Y −36.0 and Y +36.0, ± 0.05 mm, and is flat everywhere outside the letters, with no slope or bevel.
   - The letter walls stand at 90 ± 1 degrees to the face.
   - The colour-boundary edge is at Y −36.4 and Y +36.4, ± 0.05 mm, all round every letter wall and hole wall, with green in front of it and black behind.
   - The edge break round each black face is 0.6 ± 0.1 mm at 45 degrees.
5. **Walls and overhangs.**
   - Outer walls and the face walls 3.2 ± 0.1 mm. No wall or detail thinner than 1.2 mm.
   - On `BankFront_Print` and `BankBack_Print`, every face that points downwards more than 45 degrees from vertical is either a letter face on the plate (Z = 0) or part of the flat black face and its edge break at 4.0 mm above the plate. Nothing else needs support.
6. **Joint, with the halves assembled at their origins.**
   - The halves touch only on the seam faces at Y 0.
   - The lip fits inside the step with 0.20 ± 0.03 mm sideways gap and 0.30 ± 0.05 mm at its end.
   - The only overlap is the 4 ridges, each sitting in its groove with 0.1 mm free.
   - The outer surfaces of the two halves are flush across the seam within 0.05 mm.
   - The pry notch, in the front half at X 0, measures 14 x 2.4 x 1.5 mm.
7. **Slot.**
   - Seen from above, the slot is 5.0 ± 0.1 mm wide and 38.0 ± 0.2 mm long (or the length the rule in 3.2 gives, reported), centred on X 0 ± 0.5 mm and Y 0 ± 0.1 mm.
   - At least 4 mm of flat top lies beyond each end.
   - A test disc 26 mm across and 2.5 mm thick, held upright under the slot, can drop straight down into the hollow without touching anything.
8. **Clean solids.**
   - Each of the 8 objects is watertight, has no non-manifold edges, no faces passing through each other and no stray pieces.
   - Scale is 1 on every object. The names are exactly those in section 3, with no ".001".
   - `export_for_print` reports no warnings for `BankFront_Print`, `BankBack_Print` and the four test-piece objects.
   - The `_Print` objects match `BankFront` and `BankBack` in shape: the same volume within 0.1 %.
9. **Same look from both sides.** Renders 2 (`hero`) and 5 (`back-hero`) look the same apart from the seam details and the pry notch.
10. **Renders.** All 10 images, 1600 x 1200, are in `designs/aibat-bank/renders/`.

## 13. What a later version could add

- **A textured black face as well:**
  - letters as separate inserts: the black body prints with its flat face down on the plate, with pockets for the letters, and the green letters print separately face down and are pressed or glued in. Both colours get the plate texture and no supports are needed;
  - a flush two-colour face: green letters inlaid level with a flat black face, printed face down with the AMS swapping colours inside each layer;
  - the support-free 45 degree bevel of the first draft, if the support marks bother the user.
- Both halves on one plate, if the purge tower fits, to save one colour change.
- A thin white outline round the letters, or the black face in a third colour.
- An engraved line on the bottom: a date, "from ...", or a small picture.
- A one-way flap under the slot so coins do not shake out, or a round plug in the bottom instead of the split.
- Small rubber feet or a printed non-slip pad.
- Magnets in the rim instead of the click bumps, if the clicks wear out.
- A rendered plate texture on the letter faces, so the renders show the real finish.
- Another font, if Impact turns out too heavy in the renders.
