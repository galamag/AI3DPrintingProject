Status: approved by the user on 2026-11-13, straight-through outline as in the references.

**Revisions of 2026-11-13.** The user changed the first draft five times on the same day. This brief includes the result of all five.

1. **Name on both sides** (later replaced by change 5). The back half was to carry the name turned half a turn, so that it read left to right from behind.
2. **Straight letters, printed with supports.** No 45 degree bevel. Each side is a flat black face, and the green letters stand straight out of it by 4.0 mm, with upright walls. Each half prints letters down, so the flat black face is printed over supports. It will not have the plate texture and will show support marks; the user accepted this on 2026-11-13 in exchange for straight letters. The user's own point: the supports are printed during the green layers, so they come out green and cost no extra filament change or purge.
3. **Colour change two layers early.** The filament changes to black two layers before the flat black face starts, so the black face is not smeared with green from the supports. With a 4.0 mm relief and 0.2 mm layers the change is at 3.6 mm.
4. **The outline follows the letters, per side** (replaced by change 5 before it was built). The user had seen renders of a shared, left-right symmetric outline and chose outlines that follow the letters, which with change 1 meant a different outline on each half and steps along the seam.
5. **Straight through, as in the references (fifth change).** The user looked at the references again and said that there the inscription on the other side is not mirrored in position but repeats straight through: А behind А, Й behind Й, so only one side is readable. The box is redone the same way:
   - **One outline.** The box is one outline, the front word grown by 8 mm, running straight through from the front letter faces to the back letter faces. There are no seam steps, no ledges, no per-side outlines and no symmetric union.
   - **The back.** It carries the same letters straight behind the front ones, so from behind the name appears mirrored. The user accepts that only the front is readable.
   - **The halves are mirror twins.** The back half's outside is the front half's outside mirrored through the seam plane (Y = 0), so the two halves are mirror images on the outside. They differ only in the joint: the lip and the pry notch on the front half, the rebate on the back half, and half of the slot in each.
   - **Joint.** The hollow, lip, rebate, click ridges, grooves and seam bevel follow this one outline as originally designed. The test piece is unchanged.
   - **Slot.** It goes on the longest flat stretch of the top, over the second А and Т, at the full 38 mm.
   - **Measurements.** The outline numbers below were measured by the modeler on the grown word.

Unchanged: the size 220 x 100 x 80 mm, the split at Y 0, the 4.0 mm straight relief, the colour change at the relief height minus two layers, the supports under each flat black face, the 3.2 mm walls, the lip on the front half, the colours, the test piece and the measured letter numbers.

## 1. What it is

A personalised money box in the style of the name banks in the references. It is a thick black body whose outline follows the name «АЙБАТ», running straight through from front to back like a cookie-cutter shape. The front face carries the name as raised green capitals, readable left to right. Each side is a flat black face, and the five letters stand straight out of it by 4 mm, with upright walls and flat faces, like the THOMAS reference. Round the letters is an 8 mm black border that follows them:
- a hump over the short mark on Й (its "breve");
- an end that leans slightly with the first А;
- a step in under the right arm of Т;
- a deep V in the top between Б and the second А;
- a shallow dip between the first А and Й.

The back face carries the same green letters straight behind the front ones, as in the references. From behind, the name therefore reads mirrored, like the front seen through glass. A coin slot runs along the top over the second А and Т. The box splits down the middle into a front half and a back half, which click together and can be pried apart with a coin to take the money out.

**Surface finish, as accepted by the user.** Each half prints with its letter faces down on the textured plate. The green letter faces therefore get the fine plate texture. The flat black face is printed in mid-air, 4 mm above the plate, on supports that are snapped off afterwards. It comes out slightly rough and shows faint support marks, and it has no plate texture. The side walls and top show fine horizontal layer lines.

Assumptions I made where the references and the user's answers say nothing:

- **Font: Impact**, which is installed on this PC (`C:\Windows\Fonts\impact.ttf`) and has Cyrillic letters. It is the closest installed match to the THOMAS letters: upright, heavy and narrow. Fitted to 204 mm wide it needs only a slight upward stretch (1.051 times, measured), so it keeps its natural look.
- **Relief height 4.0 mm.** That is 20 layers of 0.2 mm. On 69 mm tall capitals it reads clearly from the side and in the three-quarter view and casts a visible shadow. A higher relief would make the supports taller, use more filament and make them harder to pull out of the letter holes; a lower one would look flat.
- **The 100 mm height is the top of the hump over Й.** The rest of the top is at Z 84.96 (measured).
- **The 80 mm depth is measured from the front letter faces to the back letter faces**, so it includes both 4 mm reliefs.
- **The name is centred by its width.** The green word, from the left foot of the first А (X −102.00) to the right tip of Т's top bar (X +102.00), is centred on the box.
- **Dips in the top.** The two dips wider than 6 mm are kept, because they are part of the outline following the letters: the deep V between Б and the second А, and the shallow dip between the first А and Й. The two hairline dips (Й to Б, and the second А to Т) are filled by the narrow-notch rule.
- **The hump's top is straightened** to one flat line at Z 100. The breve's concave top would otherwise leave a 0.46 mm sag.
- **A small edge break** (a 0.6 mm 45 degree chamfer) runs round the outer edge of each flat black face.
- **The coin slot is 38 mm long, over the second А and Т** (section 3.2). That is the longest flat stretch of the top.
- **Letters do not touch.** They have a 3 mm gap (measured), which shows as a thin black line of face.
- **The coin:** 200 tenge, about 26 mm across (the user's figure). I assume no coin is thicker than 2.5 mm.
- **The banknote** is folded lengthwise into thirds or quarters before it goes in, to a strip no wider than about 28 mm. This assumes a banknote about 70 mm tall, which is my estimate. The 38 mm slot also takes most notes folded only in half if they are no more than about 72 mm tall; that is an estimate too.
- **The box is opened from below.** You turn it over, put a coin into a small notch at the bottom seam and twist.

The box is **2 printed parts**: the front half and the back half. A separate **test piece of the joint** has 4 small printed parts and is printed first. ### 3.1 The body outline (one outline, straight through)

This is the shape every slice of the body follows, from the front black face to the back black face. It is the same for both halves.

1. **Letters.** «АЙБАТ» in Impact capitals, spaced so every pair of neighbours is 3.0 mm apart at its closest point, the breve in its own position over Й. Fitted as on the built model: scaled evenly to 204 mm wide, stretched upwards 1.051 times to 84 mm from the baseline to the top of the breve, baseline at Z 8, centred on X 0 (X −102.00 to +102.00).
2. **Grow** the outline of all the letters, breve included, outwards by 8 mm, with round corners.
3. **Fill** every hole inside it: the insides of А and Б, closed gaps between letters (for example under Т's left arm), and the gap between the breve and Й.
4. **Cut** it flat at Z = 0, and fill any dip in the bottom edge (under the legs of each А) up to that flat line.
5. **Small fixes**, and nothing else:
   - the top of the hump is made one straight line at Z 100 from X −49.3 to −24.5, filling the 0.46 mm sag left by the breve's concave top;
   - any notch narrower than 6 mm at its mouth is filled to the straight line across it. This fills the Й–Б dip (1.17 mm) and the second А–Т dip (4.18 mm), so the top runs flat at Z 84.96 from the hump to the deep V, and from the second А to the end of Т's bar;
   - sharp inside corners get a 2 mm round.
6. **Kept as they are:** the hump over Й, the lean of the А end, the step-in under Т's right arm, the shallow dip between the first А and Й, and the deep V between Б and the second А.

Growing an outline outwards makes no thin spikes or slivers, so there is nothing fragile to fill. Every outline edge is an upright wall when a half lies on the plate, so nothing in the outline is unprintable either.

**The outline in plain words** (seen from the front, measured):
- a flat bottom from X −102 to +91.86;
- the left end leans 8.6 mm with the side of А, from X −110 at Z 8 to −101.38 at Z 77, and rounds over to the top;
- the top at Z 84.96 over the first А (X −93.73 to −68.24);
- a shallow dip 3.15 mm deep;
- the hump over Й (X −56.41 to −17.44), 15 mm higher, flat at Z 100 for about 25 mm;
- the top at Z 84.96 over Б to X +19.58;

### 3.2 `BankFront` (front half, with the readable name and the lip)

**The front, from the letter faces back:**

- **Letter faces:** all five letters and the breve are flat, at Y −40, and parallel to the black face. They read «АЙБАТ» left to right.
- **Letter walls:** each letter outline (and its holes) runs straight back, square to the face, for exactly 4.0 mm, to the flat black face at Y −36. No bevel, no slope, no rounding on the letters; the letter face edges are crisp.
- **Colour boundary:** there must be a real edge in the mesh at Y −36.4, 3.6 mm behind the letter faces, all round every letter wall and every hole wall. The letter faces and the walls in front of this edge are green; the last 0.4 mm of the walls behind it, and everything further back, is black. This is where the filament changes in the print (section 10).
- **Flat black face:** one flat surface at Y −36, filling the outline round the letters: the 8 mm border, the 3 mm gaps between letters, the insides of А and Б, and the gap between the breve and Й. It meets the letter walls in sharp inside corners.
- **Edge break:** round the outer edge of the flat black face, where it meets the side walls, a 0.6 mm 45 degree chamfer.
- **Side wall:** the outline runs straight back from the black face to the seam at Y 0. It is 3.2 mm thick.
- **Inside:**
  - **Shape:** the hollow follows the outline, shrunk by 3.2 mm, with corners rounded to at least 2 mm. Any part of the hollow narrower than 6 mm is left solid.
  - **Hump:** the hollow reaches up into the hump as a pocket about 32.6 mm wide and 15 mm tall, wide enough to stay hollow.
  - **Dips:** under the deep V the hollow's ceiling follows the V down, so a V-shaped ridge hangs about 10 mm into the hollow. Under the shallow dip the ceiling dips about 3 mm. Neither leaves any part of the hollow narrower than 6 mm. The modeler reports any part that is.
  - **Under Т's right arm:** the hollow reaches into the arm above the step-in, which leaves a small shelf inside, at about Z 58, from about X +96.7 to +106.8.
  - **Depth:** the hollow's front face is flat at Y −32.8, so the face wall behind the flat black face is 3.2 mm thick (7.2 mm behind the letters). The hollow is open towards the back (Y 0).
- **Seam line:** the outer edge of the rim (at Y 0) has a small 0.6 mm 45 degree bevel. With the matching one on the back half it makes a neat V line, so the seam looks intended.
- **Lip:** the inner 1.4 mm of the wall continues past the seam by 4.0 mm, to Y +4.0, all the way round, so it slides into the back half. Its inner face is flush with the inside wall. Its outer face is 1.8 mm in from the outside surface.
  - **Why the lip is on the front half:** the halves are otherwise mirror twins, so the joint works either way. Keeping it on the front half keeps the joint exactly as on the test piece (`CouponFront` has the lip), keeps the pry notch beside the lip as designed, and leaves the back half 4 mm lower on the plate.
- **Click bumps (4):** small ridges on the outer face of the lip. Each is 8 mm long along the wall, with its ends tapered over 1 mm.
  - **Profile across the ridge:** it stands 0.4 mm out from the lip. It is 1.4 mm wide at the base (Y +1.8 to +3.2) and has a flat top 0.6 mm wide (Y +2.2 to +2.8), with 45 degree sides.
  - **Placement:**
    - two on the bottom wall, at X −50 and X +50;
    - one on the top wall, centred on the flat top from the hump to the deep V, at X +1.07 (about 14.5 mm of flat on each side);
    - one on the right end, on the straight upright wall under Т's arm (X +99.86, Z 8 to 55.16), centred at Z 31.6.
  - **Why not the original placement:** the original rule put the top bumps over the first А and over Т.
    - The flat top over the first А (25.5 mm) is too short to keep a bump 10 mm from its corners.
    - The slot now takes the top over Т.
    - The slot leaves the lip out along most of the top on the right, so the right end gets the fourth bump.
  - **Spacing:** each bump is at least 10 mm from a corner, the slot and the pry notch.
- **Coin slot, front half:** a notch in the top wall from Y −2.5 to the seam at Y 0, through the full wall into the hollow.
  - **Place:** on the seam, centred on the longest flat stretch of the top that is clear of the hump and the dips. After the fill rule, that is the stretch over the second А and Т's bar, X +34.64 to +102.40, 67.8 mm long. The other stretches are shorter: from the hump to the deep V 37.0 mm, over the first А 25.5 mm, and the hump's top about 25 mm.
  - **Length rule** (the original one): use 38 mm if the flat stretch is at least 46 mm long. Otherwise, use the flat stretch minus 8 mm, but never less than 32 mm. That leaves at least 4 mm of flat top beyond each end of the slot. If even 32 mm does not fit, stop and report.
  - **Result:** **38 mm**, from X +49.52 to +87.52, centred at X +68.52, with about 15 mm of flat top beyond each end. Each end is rounded (radius 2.5 mm), so the two halves together make a slot with round ends.
  - **Why this stretch:** it is the only one long enough for the full 38 mm with the 4 mm end margin. By the same rule the stretch over Б (37.0 mm) would allow only 29 mm, below the 32 mm minimum.
  - **What passes:** a 26 mm coin clears the slot by 6 mm at each end. Whatever its position along the slot, a coin stays at least 9 mm left of the shelf under Т's right arm, so it drops straight to the bottom. Banknotes folded lengthwise to 28 mm pass; notes up to about 72 mm tall folded in half pass tightly.
  - **Lip:** over the slot's length plus 2 mm at each end (X +47.52 to +89.52), the lip is left out.
- **Pry notch:** a notch for a coin, in the bottom wall at the seam, centred at X 0. It is 14 mm long (along X), 2.4 mm wide (Y −2.4 to 0) and 1.5 mm deep (Z 0 to 1.5). It is cut in the front half only. A coin pushed in bears against the back half's rim, and twisting it pries the halves apart.

### 3.3 `BankBack` (back half, with the name straight through)

- **Outside:** exactly the outside of `BankFront` from its letter faces to the seam, without its lip and pry notch, mirrored through the seam plane Y = 0. It is not turned round. So:

- **Inside:** the same hollow as the front half, mirrored, with its back face flat at Y +32.8 (a 3.2 mm face wall). It is open towards the front.
- **Rebate for the lip:** the step in the wall that takes the front half's lip. Over the first 4.3 mm behind the seam (Y 0 to +4.3), only the outer 1.6 mm of the wall stays, as a thin skirt. The inner part is removed, which leaves a 0.2 mm gap round the lip's outer face. The lip stops 0.3 mm short of the floor of the step (Y +4.0 against +4.3), so the halves meet on the outer seam and not on the lip's end.
- **Click grooves (4):** in the skirt's inner face, opposite the 4 ridges. Each is 10 mm long and 0.3 mm deep.
  - **Profile:** it is 1.6 mm wide at the face (Y +1.7 to +3.3) and has a flat floor 1.0 mm wide (Y +2.0 to +3.0), with 45 degree sides.
  - **How it clicks:** when the halves close, each ridge has to push the skirt 0.2 mm outwards and then drops into its groove.
- **Coin slot, back half:** the matching notch, from the seam at Y 0 to Y +2.5. It has the same length, ends and place as in the front half. Over the slot's length plus 2 mm at each end, the rebate is filled solid (full wall thickness), because the lip is missing there.

- **No pry notch** on the back half.

## 6. Topology (how the surface is built)

- **Type:** hard-surface solids made for printing. Each object is one closed solid: watertight, no holes in the surface, no loose or doubled faces, no parts passing through themselves, normals pointing outwards.
- **Real size:** everything is built at the sizes above, with the scale applied (reset to 1).
- **No smoothing step** (no subdivision). Curved letter outlines are made of enough short straight pieces that no facet shows in the renders: no more than 0.05 mm off the true curve.
- **Size of the mesh:** about 50,000–200,000 triangles per half is fine.
- **Thinnest parts:**
  - nothing thinner than 1.2 mm anywhere. The thinnest parts are the 1.3 mm wall behind each click groove and the 1.4 mm lip;
  - the click ridge (0.4 mm high) and the groove (0.3 mm deep) are shapes on a wall, not free-standing details.
- **Colour boundary:** on both halves, the edge 3.6 mm behind the letter faces is a real edge round every letter wall and hole wall, so the faces in front of it can carry the green.
- No animation, no rig.

## 10. Print

- **What it is for, and the load:**
  - A money box on a desk or shelf. It stands on its bottom face and holds coins and folded notes.
  - Full, it may hold about 2–3 kg of coins (estimate). The weight rests on the bottom walls of both halves.
  - The lip inside the step stops the halves from sliding against each other up and down or sideways. The 4 clicks only hold them together.
  - It is a box for keeping money, not a lock: a child can open it with a coin.
  - Pick it up full by holding both halves, not by one half alone.
- **Printed size:**
  - assembled: 220 x 80 x 100 mm (W x D x H);
  - front half on the plate: 220 x 100 mm, 44 mm tall (40 mm plus the 4 mm lip);
  - back half on the plate: 220 x 100 mm, 40 mm tall;
  - each fits the 256 x 256 x 256 mm volume with about 18 mm to spare along the long side.
- **Plates, in order:**
  1. **Test piece** (`CouponFront`, `CouponBack_015`, `_020`, `_025`).
     - Grey, slot A2, one colour, no supports.
     - Each part lies floor-down.
     - Small and quick, about 15 g (estimate).
     - Its result sets the gap before the box is exported (3.4).
  2. **Front half** (`BankFront_Print`), letter faces down, with supports and one colour change.
  3. **Back half** (`BankBack_Print`), exactly the same way.
- **For both halves:**
  - **Bed face:** only the green letter faces and the breve touch the plate (Y −40 for the front half, Y +40 for the back half). They are large and flat and get the textured-plate finish.
  - **The colour change rule:** green (A1) from the plate; the slicer changes to black (A4) **two layers below the flat black face**, that is at the relief height minus two layer heights. With 0.2 mm layers and the 4.0 mm relief this is **3.6 mm**: green for layers from 0 to 3.6 mm, black from the layer that starts at 3.6 mm to the top. The model's colour-boundary edge sits at the same 3.6 mm. If a different layer height is chosen, the change and the boundary edge move to 4.0 mm minus two of those layers, and the modeler moves the edge to match.
  - **Why two layers early (the user's reason):** the last support layers (their top interface) and the last layers of the letter walls are then already black, so the black face is not smeared with green from the supports. The 0.4 mm black at the base of each letter is not noticeable from the front.
  - **Layer height** must make both 3.6 mm and 4.0 mm layer boundaries, for example 0.2 mm layers with a 0.2 mm first layer.
  - **Supports are required** under the whole flat black face of each half, which lies 4.0 mm above the plate. That includes:
    - the border round the letters and the 3 mm gaps between them;
    - the insides of А and Б;
    - the gap between the breve and Й;
    - the face of the hump.

    All supports stand on the plate; nothing inside the box needs support. The user's point, recorded: the supports are printed during the green layers, so they come out green (with black top layers) and cost no extra filament change or purge.
  - **For the print engineer to decide:**
    - the support type (normal or tree);
    - the interface layers and their pattern;
    - the top Z gap between the support and the black face, which decides how cleanly the face releases and how rough it is;
    - whether the narrow 3 mm gaps and the small closed letter holes can be bridged instead of supported.

    Pulling supports out of the closed holes of А and Б is the awkward part of the clean-up; a support pattern that comes out in one piece helps.
  - If the slicer adds a purge tower for the colour change, the half lies so that the tower fits beside its 100 mm side.
  - Both halves change colour at the same height, so they could share one plate (about 220 x 210 mm together). Version 1 plans one plate each: then a failed plate costs only one half, and the purge tower has room. The print engineer may suggest one shared plate to the user.
- **Surface finish, accepted by the user on 2026-11-13:** the flat black faces are printed over supports, so they do not get the plate texture and will show support marks. Only the letter faces get the plate texture. This was accepted in exchange for straight letters.
- **Filament estimate** (rough; the print engineer gives the real figures). All Bambu PLA Lite, loaded as A1 green, A2 grey, A3 white, A4 black:
  - the solid volume is expected at about 320–360 cm³, close to the 340 cm³ measured on the symmetric version. The outline is about 10 % smaller in area than the symmetric one, but it has a longer edge (hump, V, step-in), so the walls weigh about the same;
  - black (A4): about 260–330 g for both halves, including the black top layers of the supports;
  - green (A1): about 45–75 g for both halves: about 30–40 g in the letters and about 15–35 g in the supports, which are thrown away;
  - grey (A2): about 15 g for the test piece;
  - white (A3): not used;
  - plus the waste of the two colour changes (purge), a few grams to about 15 g each (estimate).
- **Overhangs:** in print orientation, the only surfaces facing the plate are the letter faces (on the plate) and the flat black face with its edge break (on the supports). Everything else is upright or leans no more than 45 degrees:
  - the outline sides, the hump, the dips and the shelf under Т's arm are upright walls, because the outline runs straight through;
  - the click ridges and the sides of the grooves are at 45 degrees;
  - the slot and the pry notch are open at the top of the rim;
  - there is no ceiling inside either half.
- **Walls:** outer walls 3.2 mm, face wall 3.2 mm behind each flat black face, lip 1.4 mm, skirt 1.6 mm, at least 1.3 mm behind a groove.
- **Fits and gaps, starting values:**

  | Fit | Starting value | Tuned by |
  |---|---|---|
  | Lip to skirt, sideways | 0.20 mm | test piece: 0.15 / 0.20 / 0.25 |
  | Lip end to the floor of the step | 0.30 mm | |
  | Click ridge | 0.4 mm high, so 0.2 mm overlap when passing the skirt | |
  | Ridge sitting in its groove | 0.1 mm free | |
  | Slot | 5.0 mm wide, 38 mm long, for coins up to 26 mm across and 2.5 mm thick, and folded notes | |
  | Pry notch | 2.4 mm wide, for a coin edge | |

- **May not be changed without the user:**
  - the font, the name, the overall size, the outline following the letters straight through;
  - the straight letter walls, the 4.0 mm relief, and the colour change two layers below the black face;
  - the split at Y 0, the slot size and position, the colours.
  - Turning either half to another face defeats the design. Report instead.
- **For the print engineer:**
  - The click joint needs solid walls near the rim. Four or more wall loops make the 3.2 mm walls nearly solid.
  - The letter faces of each half must touch the plate fully: flat, not tilted.
  - Whether `slice_model` can insert the filament change at 3.6 mm and set up the supports is not known. Either may have to be set in the Bambu Studio window, for two plates. This is a question for the orchestrator and the user, not a reason to change the model.

## 13. What a later version could add

- **A slot nearer the middle (option):** over Б, on the 37.0 mm flat top between the hump and the deep V. By the length rule it allows only 29 mm, below the 32 mm minimum. It would need the end margin relaxed to about 1 mm (35 mm slot), which the user would have to accept.
- **A slot across the top (option):** a slot running front to back, 5 mm wide (in X) and up to 38 mm long (in Y), centred over Б or the second А.
  - Each half gets half of it as a notch open at the seam, so it prints without bridging.
  - The lip is interrupted for only about 9 mm.
  - It looks less like the references, which have the slot along the top.
- **Rejected alternatives, kept for the record:**
  - readable name on both sides with per-side outlines: steps and ledges along the seam and a short slot; the user preferred the references' straight-through look;
  - one symmetric shared outline with a flat top plateau: the outline no longer followed the letters, which the user rejected from its renders.
- **A textured black face as well:**
  - letters as separate inserts: the black body prints with its flat face down on the plate, with pockets for the letters, and the green letters print separately face down and are pressed or glued in. Both colours get the plate texture and no supports are needed;
  - a flush two-colour face: green letters inlaid level with a flat black face, printed face down with the AMS swapping colours inside each layer;
  - the support-free 45 degree bevel of the first draft, if the support marks bother the user.
- Both halves on one plate, if the purge tower fits, to save one colour change.
- A thin white outline round the letters, or the black face in a third colour.
- An engraved line on the bottom: a date, "from ...", or a small picture.
- A one-way flap under the slot so coins do not shake out, or a round plug in the bottom instead of the split.
- Small rubber feet or a printed non-slip pad.
- Magnets in the rim instead of the click bumps, if the clicks wear out.
- A rendered plate texture on the letter faces, so the renders show the real finish.
- Another font, if Impact turns out too heavy in the renders.
